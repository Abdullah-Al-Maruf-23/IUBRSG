Who we are

Scouting is more than an activity. It is a way to grow.

IUB Rover Scout Group brings university life and community service together. We create opportunities for students to build confidence, practice leadership, develop practical skills, care for the environment, and serve with compassion — together.

Our journey began with the group’s official launch at IUB on 27 November 2025. Since then, our activities have included outreach, environmental action, outdoor adventure, campus volunteering, skills development, and participation in the wider Scouting movement.

Our journey so far

Moments that shape our story.

From an opening ceremony to service, learning, and adventure — these milestones reflect the many sides of Rovering at IUB.

27 November 2025

The group officially launches

The IUB Rover Scout Group was formally launched at IUB, alongside the opening of the Rover Den, a foundation tree plantation, scarf unveiling, and a cultural programme.

’25
⚜️A new chapter begins
27 January 2026

Winter humanitarian outreach

A team of 10 Rovers distributed blankets and food to people spending winter nights outdoors in several areas of Dhaka.

01
🧣Service with dignity
24–26 April 2026

First Adventure Camp · Rangamati

Three days of outdoor exploration, hiking, team-building, campfire, cultural experiences, and a Kaptai Lake visit strengthened skills and friendships.

02
🏕️Learn through adventure
7 June 2026

World Environment Day action

With Environmental Trailblazers, Rovers planted 50 saplings at IUB’s Ulukhola land and distributed 500 seed papers to encourage environmental awareness.

03
🌳Plant today. Grow tomorrow.
16–24 July 2026

Welcoming new Rovers & campus service

The second batch began its Scouting journey with a crew meeting and introductory learning. Rovers also volunteered at IUB’s Autumn 2026 Admission Test, helping candidates and guardians navigate campus.

04
🧭Welcome in. Help out.
30 July 2026

Fruit Festival 2026

The Multipurpose Hall welcomed a community celebration of seasonal Bangladeshi fruits, healthy living, fellowship, and more than 25 fruit varieties, with over 80 Rovers taking part.

05
🍉Community & wellbeing
24–25 June · certificates on 30 July 2026

First Aid Training & certification

In training delivered by experienced Bangladesh Red Crescent Society instructors, participants developed practical first-aid skills. Certificates were presented to 25 trainees, including 19 IUB Rovers and 6 IUB officials.

06
🩹Skills that may save a life
31 July 2026

Skill Based Day Camp at BUET

IUB’s two male Rover units joined a Bangladesh Scouts, Dhaka District Rover event with around 155 units and 1,500+ Rovers and leaders. Activities included pioneering, camp craft, first aid, navigation, and a campfire performance.

07
🪢Skill, teamwork, and fellowship
20 September – 2 October 2026

Peace, wellbeing & safe Scouting

Public awareness work included a World Cleanup Day sustainability campaign, participation by 30 Rovers in a Mental Health First Aid workshop, volunteering at the Henrik Ibsen–Jon Fosse Festival, and four Rovers completing Safe from Harm certification.

08
💚Care for people & place
14–16 October 2026

Clean campus, stronger skills, global links

Rovers joined a campus cleanup and awareness campaign, more than 40 candidates took part in the written assessment, and four Rovers represented IUB at the JOTA–JOTI 2026 inauguration.

09
🌍Ready for the next step
