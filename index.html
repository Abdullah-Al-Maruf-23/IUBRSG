Who we are

Scouting is more than an activity. It is a way to grow.

IUB Rover Scout Group brings university life and community service together. We create opportunities for students to build confidence, practice leadership, develop practical skills, care for the environment, and serve with compassion — together.

Our journey began with the group’s official launch at IUB on 27 November 2025. Since then, our activities have included outreach, environmental action, outdoor adventure, campus volunteering, skills development, and participation in the wider Scouting movement.

Quick answers

New to Rovering?

Start with these answers, then check the current official channels for recruitment notices, event requirements, and updated contact details.

Ask through official channels ↗
What is IUB Rover Scout Group?

It is the Rover Scout group at Independent University, Bangladesh, focused on learning by doing through leadership development, community service, practical skills, outdoor activities, and wider Scouting connections.

Who can join, and when is recruitment open?

Recruitment windows and eligibility should be confirmed through current IUBRSG and university notices. The website does not collect applications or promise that recruitment is currently open.

What activities can Rovers take part in?

Public updates document campus volunteering, environmental action, humanitarian outreach, crew meetings, adventure camps, first-aid learning, Safe from Harm training, awareness campaigns, and national or international Scouting events.

Can a student attend a training or event without joining?

Access varies by event. Read the specific announcement and ask the organizers before attending. Some activities are intended for members, while others may invite the wider university community.

Where is IUB located?

Independent University, Bangladesh is located at Plot 16, Aftab Uddin Ahmed Road, Bashundhara Residential Area, Dhaka 1229. Check the university website for campus directions and access guidance.

Is this website the official source for dates and recruitment?

This is an informational website concept that summarizes public sources. For a current event date, membership rule, fee, or recruitment notice, use IUBRSG’s official channels and the university’s published information.
